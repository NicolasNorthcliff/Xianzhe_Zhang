About me

I recently completed my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at Northeastern University, working with Professor Yongmin Liu. My research explores dielectric metasurfaces, resonant light–matter interactions, and the optical detection of molecular chirality.

I enjoy moving between analytical concepts, electromagnetic simulation, cleanroom nanofabrication, and bench-top optical measurement. Beyond research, I also enjoy outdoor adventures with Coco.
